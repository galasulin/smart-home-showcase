The flagship automation

A doorbell camera that knows which way you're walking.

A single snapshot can't tell you whether someone is coming home or leaving, and that's where most "person detected" alerts go wrong. So I don't send the model one frame. I stream four frames across four seconds to Gemini, along with a written map of the hallway and a strict JSON schema. Direction comes from how the person's size changes across the frames. The answer then drives a four-way notification router.

What runs the house

Twelve systems, one nervous system.

Front-door vision AI

A 4-frame / 4-second stream analysis with Gemini, a written hallway map and an enum-constrained JSON schema. It tells a resident coming home apart from a neighbour passing by or a courier at the door, and ignores permanent objects in the corridor.

llmvision.stream_analyzer · Reolink · Frigate

Home Front Command choreography

When a siren alert hits the area, the house turns on the shelter-path lights, closes all six shutters, switches the AC off, sends critical pushes that bypass silent mode, and counts down out loud in Hebrew on the living-room speaker.

oref_alert · mode: restart · he-IL TTS

Telegram command center

Commands like /salon or /entrance, or just typing "מה קורה בסלון?", grab a live frame and get a one-line description from Gemini. Inline keyboards turn off lights, close shutters or stop the AC. Everything is locked to an allow-list of two user IDs.

telegram_bot · inline_keyboard · allow-list

Leaving-home summary

Three minutes after a phone leaves the home zone, it gets one message listing which lights are on, which shutters are open and at what %, whether the AC, lock and water heater are on, and one-tap buttons to fix each of them.

person · for: 3 min · Jinja inventory

mmWave presence fan

The bathroom fan runs on PIR + mmWave radar. It switches on only after a full minute of continuous presence that the radar confirms as body movement. It has three independent ways to switch off, so a stuck sensor can never leave it running.

Zigbee radar · timer safety net

Battery death forecast

Every sensor and the smart lock get a 30-day battery-drain trend. The house predicts the day each battery will die and warns 45 days ahead. There's also a shopping reminder, and a vacation mode for the lock battery.

statistics · 30d change · namespace()

Self-healing Zigbee

If the Zigbee bridge is down for 5 minutes, the add-on restarts itself and checks again after 2 minutes. It either reports "fixed" or escalates to a critical push that points at the USB dongle. A monthly report counts the disconnects.

hassio.addon_restart · escalate

Sun, heat & rain advisor

When there's low western sun and it's over 27°C outside, the house asks (it doesn't act) whether to close the west shutters, by voice and with a Telegram button. If rain is forecast while shutters are open, it names each open shutter.

sun azimuth · ask-don't-act

Energy watchdog

It reads the national utility's smart meter directly. At 20:00 it compares today's consumption with yesterday's and flags a jump of more than 40%. A cost view breaks electricity spend down per device.

IEC meter · power-cost-card

Backup & uptime guard

It confirms every backup, alerts on failures and cloud-upload problems, and escalates if no backup has run for 3+ days. Uptime probes watch the websites I run, with "down" and "back up" alerts.

backup · UptimeRobot

AI daily & weekly digests

Every vision event goes onto a timeline calendar. At 20:00 the house sends a short story of the day, and on Friday a weekly summary. On Sunday it sends a stats report with temperature records, door openings, alert count, network speed and the last backup.

llmvision.get_events · counters

Hebrew-native glass dashboards

12 views built by hand with mushroom cards, card-mod and layout-card. Coloured top rails give each domain its own colour, Jinja makes the cards react to state (the alert strip turns red and pulses), and a bottom nav bar keeps it one-thumb friendly on a phone.

mushroom · card-mod · navbar-card
Architecture

Local first. Cloud only where it earns its place.

Home Assistant OS runs the core. Zigbee devices never touch a vendor cloud, and cameras are decoded and analysed on the LAN with Frigate. Cloud is used deliberately: a few frames go to Gemini for vision, notifications with snapshots go out through Telegram and push, speech uses cloud TTS, and a few appliances are cloud integrations.

Engineering notes

Seven decisions worth reading about.

01 · perception

Direction comes from change over time, never from one frame

The first version described a single snapshot and got "leaving vs arriving" wrong. Now the model compares size across four frames: growing means arriving, shrinking means leaving, and the same size crossing the frame means passing by. A pet or shopping bags suggest a resident, but they don't decide direction. If it's unclear, the answer is unknown, never a guess.

02 · contracts

The model returns a schema, not prose

Location, subject and alert level are closed enums, and the temperature is 0.2. Routing reads fields, not free text. The one free-text field (the Hebrew description) is only shown to humans, never branched on.

03 · context

Let other sensors veto a false alarm

If the smart lock changed state in the last 150 s, someone at the door is almost certainly a resident. That single variable turns what used to be a nightly false "stranger at the door" into a normal note.

04 · degradation

Every AI path has a dumb fallback

If Gemini times out, the automation still sends the photo with "couldn't analyse, take a look", and it still speaks. continue_on_error is set on the AI step so a cloud hiccup never swallows an alert.

05 · ergonomics

Ask, don't act, when you might be wrong

Shutters close automatically only in an emergency. For heat, sun and rain the house proposes and a person taps a button. Automation that fights the people living in the house gets turned off, and then it helps nobody.

06 · maintainability

One notification script, one change point

Every alert goes through a single script with audience, critical and keyboard fields. Swapping a phone means editing one place instead of 60 automations.

07 · resilience

Assume sensors get stuck and radios drop

The fan has three independent ways to switch off (no presence, no body motion, a hard timer). Zigbee restarts itself and escalates with a pointer to the physical dongle if that doesn't help. Backups are audited by a separate automation, so "the backup job exited 0" is never the only proof.
